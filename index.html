Viva voce: frequently asked questions


What is turnaround time?Completion time minus arrival time: how long a process spent in the system overall.


What is waiting time?Turnaround time minus burst time: time spent in the ready queue, not running.


What is response time?First time on the CPU minus arrival time.


What is the convoy effect?In FCFS, short processes queue behind one long process and wait far longer than they should.


Which algorithm gives the minimum average waiting time?SJF (and SRTF when preemption is allowed), but both can starve long processes.


How does the time quantum affect Round Robin?A huge quantum behaves like FCFS. A tiny one gives fast response but wastes time on context switches.


What is starvation and how is it fixed?A process waits forever because others keep winning. Aging raises priority over time.


How does MLFQ work here?Three queues with quanta q, 2q and FCFS. New processes start in Q0, are demoted when they use their full quantum, and a higher queue preempts a lower one.
